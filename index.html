Who We Are

Operational experience built for mission-critical environments.

VFR Direct, LLC is a veteran-owned small business founded on more than two decades of Department of the Air Force airfield operations leadership. We bring firsthand experience in airfield management, quality assurance, emergency operations, construction coordination, training programs, regulatory compliance, and government contract execution.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the mission, reduce operational risk, communicate early, and deliver accountable results without creating unnecessary oversight for the customer.

Leadership

His background includes airfield operations leadership, contractor Quality Assurance, emergency operations, hurricane evacuation and recovery, airfield construction coordination, training program management, policy development, FAA coordination, and integration of emerging aviation technologies.

The result is a company that understands the mission, the regulatory environment, and the operational discipline required to support aviation customers.
